situation of course occurs outside of gdb; I ran gdb to see
what it could tell me.

xyscript is being run in my /home/bnewell/data/scraps
directory, the one that contains about 4,000 small files.

The command chain is simply

cd /home/bnewell/data/scraps
xyscript *

(when not using gdb) and there is the segfault.

However, if I do things this way

cd /usr/local/share/xyscript and run
./xyscript /home/bnewell/data/scraps/*

everything works (except for the overloaded tab area).

The difference is that I have
/usr/local/share/xyscript/xyscript soft linked to
/usr/local/bin/xyscript.  Why does that matter?  I don't know
as yet.  It might be relevant that the link is between
separate disk drives--- or it might not be relevant.
